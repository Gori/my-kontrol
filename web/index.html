This browser can't talk to USB devices

Installing firmware needs WebUSB, which only Chrome, Edge and Opera on a computer have. Open this page in one of them. Safari and Firefox don't support it, and neither do phones.

    Technical details 
    
          
        
    
    
    

    
    What you get
    
    
      
      
    • Scales and chords. 18 scales, chord modes, a chord you teach it, strum.
      • 
      
    • Arpeggiator and note repeat. Synced to its own tempo or to MIDI clock.
      • 
      
    • Logic and Ableton Live. Transport, track names, knobs on instrument, plug-in, volume, pan or sends. The 4D encoder moves through regions and notes.
      • 
      
    • Keys. Velocity curves, keyboard split, octave and transpose.
      • 
      
    • Controls. 8 knobs × 4 pages of MIDI CC, pitch and mod strips, pedals, 8 presets.
      • 
    
    

    
    You need
    
    
      
      
    • A Komplete Kontrol M32 and its USB cable.
      • 
      
    • Chrome, Edge or Opera on macOS, Windows or Linux.
      • 
      
    • To go back later: NI's M32 firmware updater (free from Native Instruments).
      • 
    
    

    
    If something goes wrong
    
    
    Hold Shift and Browser while plugging the keyboard in. It starts in update mode, and this page can install again. NI's bootloader is never changed, so the keyboard can always be restored.
